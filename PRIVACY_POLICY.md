# Privacy Policy for Hermes Agent

**Last Updated:** October 4, 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information your Discord application ("App") may process. This Privacy Policy explains what data our App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data in accordance with the Discord Developer Terms of Service.

\---

## 1\. Data We Collect

Our App may interact with and temporarily process the following data provided by the Discord API:

* **User Information:** Discord User IDs, usernames, and avatars.
* **Server Information:** Guild/Server IDs, channel names, and role IDs.
* **Content:** Specific message contents or commands explicitly sent to the App.

## 2\. How We Use Data

We only use the processed data to execute the App's core features and commands. Specifically:

* To respond to user commands and interactions.
* To maintain application settings or server-specific configurations.
* **No Selling of Data:** We never sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties.

## 3\. Data Retention and Storage

* **Temporary Processing:** Most data processed by the App is handled in-memory and is not persistently saved.
* **Persistent Storage:** If our App requires a database to store settings (e.g., server prefixes or configuration customizer choices), this data is retained only as long as the App is invited to your server or until you request its deletion.

## 4\. Data Deletion \& User Rights

You have complete control over your data. You can request data removal at any time by:

1. Removing (kicking/banning) the App from your Discord server.
2. Contacting the App owner directly via the support server or contact details provided below.

## 5\. Third-Party Services

Our App relies strictly on the Discord API. We do not transmit your data to any other third-party services or analytics platforms.

## 6\. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our App's functionality or legal obligations. We encourage users to check this document regularly.
